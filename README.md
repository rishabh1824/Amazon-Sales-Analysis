# Amazon Sales Insights – Product Analytics Dashboard

## Product Problem Statement

Amazon sellers and category managers struggle to quickly understand:
- Which products are driving revenue
- Which categories are declining
- Where to focus inventory and promotions

Raw sales data exists, but it is not decision-friendly.

This project solves that problem by transforming raw Amazon sales data into **actionable product insights**.

---

## Target Users

- Product Managers
- Category Managers
- Business Analysts
- E-commerce Strategy Teams

---

## Product Goal

Enable product and business teams to:
- Identify top-performing and underperforming products
- Understand category-level trends
- Make data-driven decisions on pricing, inventory, and promotions

---

## Key Product Questions Answered

- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which products should be promoted or discontinued?
- How do sales trends change over time?
- Where should marketing and inventory budgets be allocated?

---

## Solution Overview

An interactive **Product Analytics Dashboard** built using **Streamlit**, enabling users to:
- Filter sales data by time and category
- Visualize revenue and order trends
- Compare product performance
- Detect growth and decline patterns

---

## Key Metrics Tracked

- Total Revenue
- Orders Volume
- Category-wise Revenue Share
- Product Performance Trends
- Time-based Growth Patterns

---

## Impact (Simulated Business Impact)

Using this dashboard, a product team can:
- Increase revenue by prioritizing high-performing categories
- Reduce inventory costs by identifying low-performing products
- Improve campaign ROI through data-backed promotion decisions
